# SuperDrinks-Live-Project


## Brand Analysis, Campaign Development & Consumer Engagement

**Project:** SuperCreator Live Project
**Brand:** SuperDrinks
**Role:** Marketing & Brand Strategy - Live Project
**Focus Areas:** Brand Analysis | Consumer Engagement | Campaign Planning | Social Media Marketing | Brand Collaboration | Creative Strategy

---

## 1. Project Overview

As part of the SuperCreator live project, I worked on developing marketing ideas for SuperDrinks, a ready-to-drink beverage brand with a portfolio spanning fruit juices, coconut water, milkshakes and cold coffee.

The project involved looking at the brand from a consumer's perspective, identifying opportunities to strengthen its positioning, and developing creative ideas to improve product discovery, engagement and purchase interest.

My approach focused on connecting products with everyday consumer moments rather than relying only on conventional promotional communication. I explored how relatable content, interactive activities, product pairings and campus-focused activations could give consumers more reasons to notice, try and purchase SuperDrinks.

The project gave me an opportunity to apply marketing concepts to a live brand brief and develop ideas across different stages of the consumer journey, from awareness and consideration to trial and potential repeat purchases.

## 2. Key Areas of Work

### A. Brand Analysis and SWOT

**Objective:** Understand SuperDrinks' current brand proposition, identify its strengths and weaknesses, and explore opportunities for growth.

I analysed the brand's product portfolio, communication approach and potential areas of differentiation. The analysis focused on how the brand could make its products more memorable and relevant to consumers.

**Key areas explored:**

- **Strengths:** A diverse beverage portfolio, multiple flavour choices and products suited to different consumption occasions.
- **Weaknesses:** The broad portfolio could make it harder for consumers to immediately associate the master brand with one distinctive product or benefit.
- **Opportunities:** Build stronger associations with everyday breaks, campus consumption, flavour discovery and relatable social content.
- **Threats:** Competition across individual beverage categories, low switching barriers and the importance of product availability and visibility at the point of purchase.

**Key strategic insight**

SuperDrinks' product variety is both an advantage and a branding challenge. The opportunity is to make the range easier to understand by connecting products with specific consumer needs, moods and occasions.

Rather than treating every product as a separate offering, I explored ways to create a more connected brand experience across the portfolio.

**Portfolio deliverable:** SWOT analysis and brand-growth opportunity assessment.

### B. WhatsApp Marketing and Product Promotion

**Objective:** Explore direct, accessible ways to introduce SuperDrinks to potential consumers.

I worked on promotional communication for WhatsApp, with a focus on making the product message engaging and relevant to a college audience.

The communication was designed to promote the beverage range rather than simply direct people to the brand's social media page.

**My approach included:**

- Developing concise, conversational promotional copy.
- Connecting beverage choices with different preferences and consumption occasions.
- Highlighting the product range through an easy-to-understand message.
- Using a clear call to action to encourage consumers to explore the brand.
- Considering how WhatsApp groups could support initial product awareness and discovery.

The objective was to make the message feel natural in a college setting while still giving consumers a clear reason to consider the products.

**Portfolio deliverable:** WhatsApp promotional messaging and consumer-facing communication concept.

### C. Market Launch Plan — SuperDrinks Coffee

**Campaign concept: COFFEE 911**

**Campaign line:** *College life has emergencies. Coffee has solutions.*

For the coffee launch task, I developed a campaign concept that connects SuperDrinks Coffee with relatable college situations.

Instead of presenting cold coffee through a conventional product advertisement, the idea uses everyday student challenges—deadlines, presentations, early classes and group-project stress—as the starting point for the communication.

The campaign turns these moments into playful “coffee emergencies”, giving the product a recognisable role within the student's daily routine.

#### The campaign idea

**COFFEE 911** is designed as a fictional, playful coffee-response concept.

Students identify their current situation and receive a suggested coffee pairing or recommendation.

Example campaign cases:

- **THE DEADLINE:** Assignment due. Brain gone.
- **THE CHAOS:** Five things happening at once.
- **THE REWARD:** Survived the day. Deserve something.

The concept can be adapted to the brand's Classic, Hazelnut and Caramel cold coffee variants.

#### Digital promotion

I developed a short-form Instagram Reel concept built around a simple narrative:

1. A student struggles with an unfinished presentation.
2. A friend introduces SuperDrinks Coffee.
3. The student takes a coffee break and returns to work.
4. The Coffee 911 campaign appears with the question, “What's your emergency?”

The creative direction prioritised natural campus settings, realistic student behaviour, smooth transitions, subtle background music and authentic product presentation.

The intention was to make the Reel feel like relatable student content rather than an overly polished advertisement.

#### Offline activation

I also developed the concept of a Coffee 911 desk within a college environment.

The proposed activation includes:

- A branded coffee-response station.
- Product display and sampling.
- A flavour recommendation guide.
- An “Emergency Board” where students write relatable college problems.
- Opportunities to convert student responses into social media content.

This creates a connection between physical participation and digital engagement.

**Portfolio deliverables:** Coffee 911 campaign concept, market launch plan, Instagram Reel brief and proposed campus activation.

### D. Brand Collaboration Pitch — The Crunch Match

**Proposed collaboration:** SuperDrinks × Beyond Snack
**Campaign:** THE CRUNCH MATCH
**Tagline:** *Sip. Crunch. Repeat.*

For the brand's “Next Big Move” pitch, I explored a collaboration between SuperDrinks and Beyond Snack, a banana-chip snack brand.

The idea came from a simple consumer behaviour insight: drinks and snacks are often purchased and consumed together, particularly during college breaks, work breaks, travel and casual hangouts.

Instead of suggesting a collaboration based only on shared branding, I developed a concept around creating complementary drink-and-snack pairings.

#### The core idea

The Crunch Match proposes limited-edition **Break Boxes**, each containing one SuperDrinks beverage and one Beyond Snack product.

Illustrative pairings include:

- **Mango Juice × Peri Peri Banana Chips** — Sweet meets spicy.
- **Classic Cold Coffee × South Indian Masala Banana Chips** — Smooth meets savoury.
- **Wild Berry Juice × Hot & Sweet Chilli Banana Chips** — Fruity meets tangy.
- **Coconut Water × Original Banana Chips** — Fresh meets classic.

The final pairings would be validated through product tasting and consumer feedback before implementation.

#### How the concept could work

The collaboration would introduce a simple pairing experience through co-branded packs, point-of-sale displays and social media communication.

Consumers could discover their preferred combination through a “Find Your Crunch Match” prompt, while the limited-edition Break Box would offer a convenient way to purchase both products together.

#### Business objective

The proposal is designed to explore four potential growth opportunities:

**1. Cross-trial:** Existing customers of either brand get an opportunity to discover the other.

**2. Bundle purchases:** A combined offer could encourage consumers to purchase a drink and snack together instead of buying only one product.

**3. New consumption occasions:** The collaboration positions SuperDrinks as part of a complete snack break rather than an isolated beverage purchase.

**4. Social sharing:** Unexpected flavour combinations provide a simple content hook for consumer reactions, recommendations and social media posts.

#### Target audience

The primary audience is college students and young Gen Z consumers who enjoy convenient snacks, explore different flavours and frequently purchase refreshments between classes or during social occasions.

The concept can also extend to young working professionals and other consumers looking for convenient snack-and-drink combinations.

**Portfolio deliverables:** Brand collaboration concept, pairing strategy, target audience definition, sales rationale and campaign visual direction.

*The Crunch Match is a proposed collaboration concept, not a confirmed commercial partnership or a measured sales outcome.*

## 3. Creative and Strategic Approach

Across the project, I focused on making marketing ideas relevant to how consumers actually behave.

Three principles guided my approach:

**1. Start with the consumer moment.**
Whether it was a coffee break or a snack craving, the ideas began with a familiar situation that consumers could recognise.

**2. Give the product a clear role.**
Instead of promoting a product in isolation, I explored how SuperDrinks could become part of a routine, occasion or experience.

**3. Connect creativity with a business objective.**
The campaign concepts were developed with clear intended outcomes, including product discovery, consumer trial, engagement and potential bundled purchases.

I also explored visual directions that prioritised product visibility, clear messaging, realistic settings and consistent brand presentation.
